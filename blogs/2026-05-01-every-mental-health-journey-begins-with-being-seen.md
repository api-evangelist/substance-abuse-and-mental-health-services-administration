Every year, tens of millions of Americans experience mental illness—but too many still struggle to be seen, heard, or connected to the care they need. Mental Health Awareness Month is a time to recognize this reality and act on it. Working in the behavioral health field, we know that when someone is going through a crisis, it can be difficult to know how to support them.
