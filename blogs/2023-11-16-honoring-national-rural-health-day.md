On November 16, we observe National Rural Health Day, a time to recognize the approximate 65 million people, or one in five Americans , living in rural America and the potential barriers to healthcare they might face. While rural areas have more land area, fewer people, scenic landscapes, and a strong sense of community, residents often face barriers in accessing healthcare services needed to reduce disparities between rural and urban health outcomes.
