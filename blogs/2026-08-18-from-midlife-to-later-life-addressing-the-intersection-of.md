For many men, midlife and the years that follow bring significant change — retirement, loss of a spouse or close friend, chronic pain, and changing hormones. These transitions can heighten vulnerability to depression, anxiety, and substance misuse, yet the signs may be difficult to recognize or discuss. Bringing greater attention to these challenges can help men recognize when they may need support and understand that help is available and recovery is possible — at all ages.
